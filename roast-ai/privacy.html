Privacy Policy RoastByAI

Effective Date: August 2026

Welcome to RoastByAI. Your privacy and data transparency are core priorities. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Process

Image & Photo Data: Images you capture or select are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS directly to Google Gemini vision AI endpoints strictly for real-time comedic analysis. We do not store, harvest, sell, or permanently retain your photos on any server. Images are deleted immediately after the roast response is generated.

Device & Advertising Identifiers: We collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic logs and Google Advertising ID (AAID) through Google Play Services to serve personalized ads, track crash analytics, and fulfill subscriptions.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with industry-standard third-party providers who process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini AI: Multimodal visual processing and text generation.
  • Google AdMob: Serving banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Monitoring app performance and stability.

3. Data Security & Retention

All network communications between the mobile application and AI processing endpoints are protected with industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.

4. Children\'s Privacy
