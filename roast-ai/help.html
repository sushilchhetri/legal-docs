Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Roast work?

Our vision model inspects visual cues in your image (facial expression, fashion, setting) and crafts customized roasts tailored to your selected persona.

How do I unlock more credits / tokens?

You can purchase Roast Pro for unlimited access or earn bonus free credits by watching short reward ads inside the app.

Are my uploaded photos stored or shared?

No. Your photos are sent securely via HTTPS strictly for real-time analysis and are permanently discarded immediately after your roast is generated.
