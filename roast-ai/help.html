💡 Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Roast analysis work?

RoastByAI scans key visual elements (facial expressions, posture, outfit, and background) using Google's vision models to generate witty, humor-driven roasts matched to your chosen persona.

Are my uploaded photos stored on your servers?

No. Your images are transmitted via secure HTTPS solely for instant AI inference. Images are never permanently saved, indexed, or shared with third parties, and are discarded immediately after processing.

How do I manage or cancel my PRO subscription?

Subscriptions are securely managed through Google Play. To modify or cancel your plan: open the Google Play Store app > tap your Profile Icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > select RoastByAI.

I purchased PRO but my premium features didn't unlock.

Make sure you are signed into the Google Play account used during checkout. Try reopening the app while connected to the internet—the app will automatically query and restore your active entitlement from Google Play.

How do I earn free roasts?

Free users receive daily tokens and can unlock extra bonus roasts by watching brief rewarded video ads directly within the app.
