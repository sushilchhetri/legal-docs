Help & Support

How does the AI Roast work?

Our AI analyzes key visual elements in your uploaded photo—including expressions, outfit, grooming, and posture—to generate personalized comedy roasters based on your chosen persona.

How do I get more tokens?

You can unlock unlimited generations by subscribing to Roast Pro, or claim free bonus tokens by watching rewarded ads in the app.

Are my photos stored or shared?

No. Images are processed in real-time solely to generate your roast output. We never store, sell, or train models on your uploaded personal photos.
