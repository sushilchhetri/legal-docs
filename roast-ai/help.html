Help & Support

How does the AI Roast work?

Our AI inspects visible elements in the photo (facial expression, fashion, vibe) and crafts custom comedy roasts based on your selected persona.

How do I get more tokens?

You can unlock unlimited generations with Roast Pro or earn bonus tokens by watching reward ads directly in the app.

Are my photos stored or shared?

No. Photos are processed strictly in real-time to generate your roast and are not permanently saved on any servers.
