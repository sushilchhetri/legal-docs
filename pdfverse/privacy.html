Privacy Policy PDFverse

Effective Date: October 2026

PDFverse is a PDF reader and editor made by The Sushi Apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles when you use it on Android or iOS. In short: your documents never leave your device, and there are no accounts.

1. Your Documents & Files

Your PDFs and scans are processed only on your device. Reading, editing, signing, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, protecting, unlocking, watermarking, filling forms and scanning all happen locally on your phone. We do not upload, store, read, or share your documents, and we have no servers that receive them.

Text recognition (OCR) and document scanning use Google ML Kit, which runs on your device. Your images and text are not sent to us.

Passwords you enter to protect or unlock a PDF are used only for that file, on your device, and are never stored or sent anywhere.

2. Permissions We Ask For

  • Files / All files access (Android): lets the Files tab find and open the PDFs on your phone and save the files you create. It is used for nothing else.
  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a document. Photos stay on your device.
  • Internet: used to show ads, process purchases, and download fonts for the editor (see below).

3. Information Collected Automatically

Advertising: The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, if you allow it), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. Premium users see no ads.

Fonts: When you edit text, the app may download a matching font from Google Fonts. Only the font name is requested; none of your document content is sent.

Saved on your device only: your settings, recent files, and premium status are stored locally in the app and are removed when you uninstall it.

4. Purchases & Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The store only tells the app whether your subscription is active.

5. Third-Party Services

These providers process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads) and Google's consent tool: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google ML Kit (on-device scanning and text recognition)
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store (purchases): Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts (font downloads)

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Remove all app data by uninstalling the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete personal data. Because we keep no account or document data, most requests concern our ad partners; contact us and we will help.

7. Children's Privacy

PDFverse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
