Privacy Policy AstroSkyra

Effective Date: October 2026

AstroSkyra is an astrology app (horoscope, Kundli, Panchang, tarot, numerology, Milan and palm reading) made by The Sushi Apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles when you use it on Android or iOS. In short: your profile and readings stay on your device, there are no accounts, and anything sent off the device is sent only to make a feature you chose work.

1. Your Profile & Readings

Stored only on your device: your name, birth date, birth time and birth place, zodiac sign, Western/Vedic preference, saved Kundli charts, Milan details, tarot history, numerology inputs, Panchang city and settings such as the daily reminder time. We have no servers that store them.

Calculated on your device: daily horoscopes, Kundli, Panchang, numerology, tarot and Milan are computed locally on your phone.

Uninstalling the app, or Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete my data, removes all of it.

2. AI Readings (optional)

AI horoscope and AI palm readings are optional and unlocked by Premium or by watching a rewarded ad. When you request one:

  • AI horoscope: your zodiac sign and the reading period are sent and, for your own sign, your birth date and time to personalise it. Your name and birth place are never sent.
  • AI palm reading: a photo of your palm is sent only after you agree on the consent screen. Palm scans without AI are analysed on your device. You can withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • A random install ID, not linked to your identity, is sent to apply a fair daily usage limit.

These requests go through our relay on Cloudflare Workers to Google's Gemini API. The relay keeps only a daily usage counter per install ID, deleted the next day; it does not store your photos, prompts or readings. AI readings are saved on your device until the day or month they cover is over.

3. Permissions We Ask For

  • Camera: used only on the palm reading screen to scan your palm.
  • Notifications: for the optional daily horoscope reminder, scheduled on your phone. Turn it off in the app's Settings or your phone's settings.
  • Internet: for AI readings, ads, purchases and checking for app updates.

The app does not ask for your GPS location, contacts, microphone or photo library. The home-screen widget only shows readings prepared on your device.

4. Information Collected Automatically

Advertising: The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, and a few cards labelled "Sponsored". AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, if you allow it), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. No ads are shown in your first days after install, and Premium users see no ads.

App updates: the app reads a small public file from GitHub Pages to learn whether a newer version is available. No personal information is sent.

5. Purchases & Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The store only tells the app whether your subscription is active.

6. Third-Party Services

These providers process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini API (AI readings): Gemini API Terms
  • Cloudflare (AI relay): Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (ads) and Google's consent tool: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store (purchases): Apple Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information. Data sent off the device travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Use the app without AI features; they are optional.
  • Decline or withdraw the palm photo consent in Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), or go Premium to remove ads.
  • Delete all app data in Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete personal data. Because we keep no account or profile data on servers, most requests concern our ad partners; contact us and we will help.

8. Children's Privacy

AstroSkyra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
